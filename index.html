黄裕洪Yuhong Huang

汕头大学商学院Business School, Shantou University

目前任职于汕头大学商学院，硕士生导师，主要从事数字经济、公司治理与资本市场领域研究。
I am currently a faculty member and master's supervisor at the Business School of Shantou University. My research focuses on digital economy, corporate governance, and capital markets.

我的研究关注新兴技术、制度变迁以及治理机制如何影响企业行为与市场结果。
My research examines how emerging technologies, institutional changes, and governance mechanisms influence corporate behavior and market outcomes.
